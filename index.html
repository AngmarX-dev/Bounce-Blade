HOW TO PLAY

1. The Blade launches automatically from the left.

2. Move your mouse or drag your finger up/down to steer the glowing Shield on the left wall.

3. The blade ricochets off enemies and returns to you.

4. Catch the Blade — if it slips past your shield, you lose a life.

5. Destroy all enemies to clear the stage.

⟡ Hitting enemies without catching = higher combo score.
